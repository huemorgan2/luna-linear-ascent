What these numbers mean

Read the complete model ↗

This is an executable proposal, not the production combat resolver. Days assume the configured attendance, sessions, energy recovery and action speed. Floors are available as personal readiness permits; shared-world gate waiting is excluded.

Weapons and strategies have unequal advantages. The simulator reports those differences. Bosses use direct attacks and protection; shared stun, poison and other future boss effects are outside this first model. Statistical difficulty cannot establish whether a fight feels fun.
