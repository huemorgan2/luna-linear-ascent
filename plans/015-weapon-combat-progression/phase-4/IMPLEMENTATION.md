# Phase 4 implementation decisions

Pre-execution record, 13 September 2026. Phase3's browser gate must close before these runtime edits start. This extends the already committed phase4 plan; it does not declare the full redesign complete.

## Evidence and root causes

The0.114.5 candidate has64 owned-weapon/source/art records and425 distinct creature profiles, but `groups._strike` still maps techniques in Python, omits Runestring's arrow advantage and ignores earned mastery. `_enemy_phase` immediately ticks newly applied poison/burn/bleed, contrary to the approved next-phase rule. Kill credit is read from the active weapon rather than the effect source. `handle` overwrites every later arrival with Cover, removing arrival variety. Only two of the eight approved resource sites exist. `gen_wiki.py` still derives legacy creature stats and imports a separate proposed model; its refreshed version stamp does not make that model the candidate resolver. These are missing phase4 work, not reasons to invent improved simulation results.

No emergency or production mitigation has been taken. The existing candidate runs solely in isolated QA. Preserve all current earned state, rolled rewards, offers and accepted expeditions.

## Changes and explicit decisions

1. Put machine-readable technique/effect/arrow definitions in the game catalog. The resolver, six-choice arrow UI, tool text and generated wiki read those records. Retain the sixteen unequal family factors and the six affinity/reach matchups. There is no requirement for equal win rates.
2. Effects snapshot the applying instance, path, resolved attack, channel and exhaustion once. Tick only effects present before the accepted action. Reapplication refreshes that source's duration without adding a same-action tick. No newly applied poison/burn/bleed tick until the following enemy phase. One source/effect stack; bleed is one strongest wound per defender. Push/stun/slow/Expose keep their documented immediate control semantics. Expire dead-target effects, preserve group cooldowns, and credit a DoT/riposte kill to its source. Resolve kill XP before final death settlement; a mutual final death forfeits haul. Reads, invalid actions and ammo selection do not tick clocks or spend energy.
3. Six arrow types are real finite supplies: Ordinary, Arcane, Poison, Fire, Pinning, Concussive. A quiver contains at most400 arrows across grades/types and sits outside the three weapon cells. Each shot uses one arrow of the bow's grade; payload costs are expressed in gold, not extra arrows per shot. New characters and existing candidate characters lacking the new quiver receive100 Common Ordinary arrows once by an enrollment receipt. Existing ordinary battles already in progress keep their prior rules. Buy20-arrow bundles at the Forge; initial price `ceil(5 × income_pillar(grade start) × payload units)`, using catalog units1/2/2/3/2/3. Grade access matches equipment access. A Common practice bundle of20 Ordinary costs1 energy and is available only when Common Ordinary stock is below20, preventing a no-money dead end without free infinite combat supply. Arrow selection is free during a group; purchasing, crafting or restocking is locked. Invalid/unreachable shots spend nothing; an accepted miss spends one arrow and weapon wear. Arcane changes direct channel, fire retains Power impact and applies later Magic burn, and Runestring gains25% Arcane impact. No hidden fallback to a different payload when selected stock is empty.
4. Expose full and current weapon contribution separately. Nonstarter contribution follows remaining/max condition; Common starters retain at least half their contribution until broken. Broken weapons cannot attack. This restores the documented purpose of repairing a damaged discovery. Keep phase3's explicit upgrade rule of adding the maximum-condition increase while preserving existing wear, except a broken weapon stays broken and still needs repair. This supersedes the older fraction-preservation wording. Record this economy change independently in matched measurements; do not relabel existing source-pinned runs.
5. Preserve School's rank0–10 consistency and earned mastery. Every earned study retains10% global attack, bow Long Draw has its earned10% Cover critical opportunity, staff Focus improves the Ground Magic partial answer to0.75 without removing the Air Power/Magic weaknesses, and blade Riposte returns25% of its resolved attack after meaningful absorption at Ground Contact. Riposte must use a selected blade instance, its condition and Power channel; it is not free anti-air. Preserve race energy/XP/armor and relevant buffs/consumables. Update candidate School descriptions where an old slot or class-action promise no longer describes the current three-weapon actions.
6. Snapshot an arrival gap for every generated member and show it in the preview. First-ever opening remains Cover; subsequent arrivals retain their rolled/role-biased Contact/Near/Far/Cover instead of being overwritten. Air favors closeness and pursuit; ground prey retains more opening distance. Ordinary/deep groups retain2–6 ranges and minimum2. Deep uses1 energy per enemy, greater specimen danger and separate rarity premiums. Existing offers retain their authored gaps/rewards. No deck-dependent counter-spawning.
7. Add the remaining six named sites from EXECUTION-DECISIONS.md at18/25/45/55/70/80. Their paid tools precede grade access, occupy utility storage, and have distinct yield/risk/durability/price records. Use nearby existing creature identities/images with weighted, disclosed bow- or blade-favored compositions; retain complementary threats. Every site snapshots its rules on entry, uses actual group combat, and secures haul only on extraction. Measure targeted return with the existing real-engine probe; do not assume all sites deserve identical probabilities.
8. Generate the public wiki from `collection`, `bestiary`, `gathering` and `workshop`. Keep all425 creature images,64 unique weapon drawings, current per-specimen/deep stats, individual rarity rates, bundle quantities, source level/condition/gates and exact upgrade quotes. Label legacy comparison and unreleased shared-warden settings explicitly. Remove duplicate JavaScript combat/economy calculations wherever authoritative tables can be generated. Game icons/16px VGA font, visible selectors up to six options, grade frames and responsive cards remain the presentation contract.

## Compatibility and verification

Pin existing group offers/active groups to their0.114 resolver while new groups use the completed phase4 resolver. Retain that reader until phase7 migration/drain verification; do not reinterpret a saved poison, arrowless shot or already-rolled haul. New expedition snapshots preserve prior yields. Add receipts for one-time quiver enrollment, not whole-player snapshot restoration.

Targeted tests must cover each effect's first/last tick, switching/source attribution, refresh/immunity, mixed-channel resistance, control recovery, final mutual death, miss/invalid ammo consumption, no-free-reload, empty/quiver-cap/grade gates, current condition/repair/broken upgrade, mastery/race, all8 site access/tool/extraction paths, preserved old offers, and425/64 canonical wiki coverage. Keep exact expected amounts in small fixtures. Run the existing full plugin/service/simulator suites after targeted checks, identifying the eight established legacy test failures separately until phase7 resolves them. Run fresh source-pinned serial/parallel/replay comparisons.

Browser scenarios S04/S05/S07/S12/S15: first query “show me the arrows I can use with this bow”; select Fire and Arcane against contrasting monsters, observe correct pixel resistance and later ticks; demonstrate push→bow/escape and source-persistent poison; show different arrival distances; visit a later named site and extract; move the wiki floor/grade controls and inspect actual differing images and drop percentages. Use declared fixtures for late floors and preserve the natural opening character. Compare prepared correct/wrong decks without imposing equal outcomes.
