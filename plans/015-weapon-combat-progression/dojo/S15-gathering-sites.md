# S15 — Named gathering sites and targeted progression

## Preconditions

Candidate floors3/18/25/45/55/70/80, owned eligible tools/decks, real resource ledgers, deterministic edge-case fixtures and a separate fresh ordinary-resource account. Local and HTTP backends, desktop/mobile and multi-turn Luna available.

## Scenario

Read the gate/map and find Drowned Copse/Bog-Iron Field by name. Inspect resource, tool, danger and favored weapons; attempt with wrong/missing/broken tool. Buy the real starter tool, gather, encounter a themed group, clear it, gather again and extract. Repeat with exactly one energy, zero energy, a broken tool, flee, death, in-place revive, refresh and duplicate extraction from two tabs. Reach high-grade sites with previous-grade equipment and inspect their fitting monsters. Compare hunting-only, prepared gathering and wrong-deck gathering in actual-engine runs. Deposit some earned gold, advance time through ordinary play, collect Vault returns and fund a later upgrade.

## Expected behavior

Gate visibility teaches that the site exists. Each real attempt spends one energy; each begun ambush enemy separately spends one or is exhausted, with clear preview text. Targeted materials and ambush bonuses remain unbanked until safe extraction. Loss affects this expedition, not prior secured materials. Tools are separate from the three weapon slots. Better preparation yields a measurable net material/upgrade advantage, with hunting still useful for gold/XP. Vault returns follow the actual engine once, not synthetic simulation income.

## Fail conditions

Hidden sites, a generic mine label replacing place identity, tool/counter acquisition circularity, fourth combat weapon, free gathering at zero energy, duplicate costs/rolls/claims, surprise unexplained ambush charge, free town heal between enemies, automatic bank on refresh, loss of prior materials, identical enemy compositions/returns everywhere, or simulator-only rewards not present in the game.

## Verify

Record source hashes, gate/site/ambush/Forge screenshots, attempted/engaged/settled receipt IDs, energy, tool wear, secured/lost material counts and Vault transactions. Show per-energy/per-minute net materials and whole-recipe times with death/tool/travel cost. Inspect matched/fresh seeds and both local/HTTP behavior. Record PASS/FAIL and timings in the numbered phase results folder before closure.
