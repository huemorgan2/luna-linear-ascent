## Preconditions

Phases 5,7. New source-pinned group-engine runs, matched-strategy validation and a deliberately short censored run; actual shared-service run when available. Raw files and replays retained.

## Scenario

Open the simulator, inspect imports/settings, run or open the same config at1and automatic workers, compare median/fastest hover points and identities, next-floor intervals, reached counts and long-range gaps. Inspect a strategy trace and replay; inspect the boss count/HP graph or its unmeasured state.

## Expected behavior

Whole-group capability and sustained farming are distinct. Days/floor/strategy are stated plainly. Missing median stays missing. Extra days use per-player intervals, fastest is observed not proven optimal, and boss counts exist only for actual service trials.

## Fail conditions

Proposal resolver as production result, resources leaked from probes, zero days for missing points, smoothed invented curve, subtracting fastest envelope points for per-player delays, reranked holdout winner, or fabricated party counts.
