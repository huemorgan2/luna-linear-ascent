# Inspect stronger player paths

## Preconditions

Local simulator serving current code, completed candidate and independent validation runs. No production data.

## Scenario

Open the winning profile and validation summary. Read the ordinary-language strategy, schedule, seed counts and limits. Select its cohort, point to early and late floor points on median and fastest lines, and inspect the fastest player's actual choices. Compare a weaker valid strategy using matched settings. Replay a traced winning player. Check missing values and narrow/wide layouts.

## Expected behavior

Every claimed speed has saved actual-engine evidence. The fastest observed line names the player; typical progression includes unfinished players. Search and held-out validation are distinguished. Historic broken decisions are not relabeled as the new baseline. All account state is synthetic.

## Fail conditions

Copied game math, free resources, RNG peeking, repeated refusal/decision loops, fastest luck claimed as guaranteed optimal, hidden incomplete players, source mismatch, clipped labels or replay mismatch.

## Verify

Match UI to run/search JSON, confirm engine fingerprint and legal action receipts, record full suite and per-scenario browser verdicts with screenshots.
