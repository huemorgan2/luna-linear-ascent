ROOTHOLLOW · TURNING IN

Where do you sleep?

Sleep is the only thing that mends wounds before dawn does — and the only way to hurry the energy bar.

Awake, returns 1 point every 45 min and wounds wait for dawn. Sleep runs both clocks:

· THE LODGE — 1 point every 22.5 min (×2, double the waking pace) and a full HP bar mends in about 2 hours. The palisade keeps ambushers off you — a bunk costs 10 carried coin.

· THE FIELDS — free. 1 point every 30 min (×1.5) and a full HP bar mends in about 4 hours. You sleep rough — anyone hunting the fields can still find you.

Wake whenever you like — the meters bank what the clock earned.
