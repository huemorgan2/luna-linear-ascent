[02] DAMAGE & COUNTERS

COLLECTION RULES

Ground or Air decides reach. Common, Power or Magic decides resistance.

The preview isolates 100 reference weapon attack against 20 DEF. It excludes body attack, training and active techniques. Air Power disperses spells: ordinary arrows are its explicit counter.

[03] WEAPONS & ARROWS

COLLECTION RULES

16 families. Four grades. 64 distinct weapon drawings, each with upgrades +0 to +20. Open a weapon to compare its four designs side by side.

ARROW PAYLOADS

Every bow accepts every payload. Runestring adds 25% impact to arcane arrows. Fire starts with a Power hit and adds a separate Magic burn. Use arrows of the bow’s grade. Buy bundles of20 at the Forge; the quiver holds400 arrows outside your three weapon slots. Each accepted shot consumes one arrow, including a miss. New players receive100 Common Ordinary arrows. A1-energy practice bundle prevents an empty-purse dead end.

[04] POWERS & EFFECTS

COLLECTION RULES

A technique rewards timing. A known weakness rewards preparation.

Effects start on a landed hit. Damage over time uses the attacker's snapshotted attack, not the monster's maximum HP. New effects tick from the next enemy phase; reapplying refreshes duration without stacking immediate ticks.

Most techniques recharge after three subsequent accepted combat actions; stun takes four. An immune target stays immune. An effect never bypasses its immunity or recovery. Upgrading a weapon does not extend its stun.

[05] DISTANCE & DEFENSE

COLLECTION RULES

CREATE AN OPENING

An enemy pursues two steps when its speed exceeds yours by at least3; otherwise one. A push delays pursuit for this phase, giving the next player action an opening. A bow shot or escape attempt still costs an action.

RETREAT & ESCAPE

Pull back creates two steps when you are faster, otherwise one. The enemy then pursues unless an effect prevents it.

Escape: clamp(10%,95%,20% + 15% × gap + 3% × speed difference). Failure consumes the action and allows a counter.

SHIELD ABSORPTION

At least 25% of a landed hit reaches HP. Only the shield's absorbed share wears its endurance. A dodge or stun prevents the hit altogether.

[07] SHARED WARDENS

ILLUSTRATIVE · NOT LIVE

One health bar that keeps healing. Actual attacks, at the same time.

Illustrative encounter: 4,500 HP, 300 HP/s healing, 10 DPS per player at 100% efficiency, 30 seconds of usable energy. These are not Floor 100 stats.

Smart counters and good coordination let a smaller group win. Keep that advantage. Use fixed healing targets rather than scaling the boss to cancel better equipment. Replace milestone pledges with accepted real attacks.

[08] CREATURE LOOT & HUNT SETTINGS

ACTUAL DROP RULES

All 425 hunt creatures, all 100 floors. Every percentage is the chance after defeating the selected creature and specimen.

Materials: four independent rolls, so several grades can drop together. Weapons: one roll, at most one weapon. “No weapon” is the rest of that roll. A group secures these drops only after every enemy is defeated.

RARITY FORMULAS, CARRIERS & DEEP-HUNT BONUSES

Deep hunts exist from Floor 4. They exclude frail/feeble prey, cost1 energy per enemy when it begins, add 20% enemy ATK and +1 speed, and favor tougher specimens. Species are chosen by their authored weights; the first tutorial group is fixed. Neither your deck nor repeated preview refreshes reroll a counter roster. Wardens use shared encounter rewards and are excluded from per-creature hunt rolls.

[09] EVERY WEAPON'S STARTING SETTINGS

COLLECTION RULES

A bought weapon arrives prepared. A monster's weapon arrives damaged. Craft a weapon in the Forge to enter a new grade without waiting for an item drop. All 64 variants have explicit source, starting level and condition settings.

Legendary shop weapon: +6, full endurance, shop unlock at Floor 84. Legendary monster drop: +0, 10% endurance. It can be found from Floor 50, but its Floor 76 equipment gate still protects progression. Forge crafting is available at Floor 76: +0, full endurance, paid in gold and materials.

Forge crafting starts at +0 with full endurance on Floors 1/26/51/76. It consumes the base gold and paired materials shown for that weapon. This is the reliable grade-transition route before higher-level shop stock unlocks. Shop prices are the Forge’s actual prepared-item quote for the delivered level; the price is paid once. Materials are included in the purchased weapon; later upgrades use materials. Attack columns show full-condition weapon bonus. Damaged drops need repair; their current endurance is shown separately from maximum.

Drop grade probabilities come from the creature loot table. Within a grade, Air favors bows, Ground Magic favors blades and Ground Power favors staves at 3× family weight; Ground Common uses weight 1. Individual weapon chance = that creature's grade chance × family weight / total eligible family weight. Equal rank is not equal power across rarities.

[10] GATHERING LOCATIONS

Eight places to collect specific upgrade materials. Buy the utility tool at the location, choose three weapons and decide when to extract. One energy per collection attempt; each ambush enemy costs its own energy when engaged.

[11] RULES & PROGRESSION

THREE WEAPONS · ONE GROUP

Choose any three owned weapons before a fight or expedition. Species keep their own images and neighboring environments. Air determines reach; Power and Magic determine resistance. Weapon techniques and arrows create different ways to win.

Earn XP per kill. Gold, materials and dropped weapons remain pending until you clear the group alive. Unpaid enemy starts latch half attack and two less speed. Retreat preserves XP and forfeits that group’s haul.

School training and mastery remain. Extra weapon-slot lessons are removed: all three slots are available from the start.

PROGRESSION IS MEASURED

There are100 floors, a character cap of30 and weapon levels+0–20 in four grades. Weapon upgrades replace the old honing multiplier.

Good counters, preparation, gathering and investment should reward smart play. The actual-game simulator measures fastest and median days to full-group capability, with reach counts and failed paths preserved. A smooth-looking graph is not evidence by itself.

This build is being verified before release. Shared Warden calculations above are explicitly illustrative; they are not the finished shared-service implementation.
