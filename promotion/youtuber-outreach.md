# YouTuber outreach

YouTubers do not have a single “submit your game” portal. They find games on **itch.io / IndieDB / Keymailer / PlayIndies**, or they take a short email with a play link.

Linear Ascent’s advantage: **it looks like a simple retro game, and it takes thousands of players together to take the tower.** Free, playable in 30 seconds. The fight can finish on camera.

## Pitch email (paste)

Subject: Linear Ascent — a simple retro game that takes thousands of players to beat

Hi {name},

Linear Ascent looks like the good old times: low resolution, simple text-based D&D. The world underneath is deep. And it takes the massive effort of thousands of players, together, to take one tower.

Free, nothing to install, about 30 seconds to start. The whole player base is hitting the same monster — a fight can actually finish on stream.

Play: https://linearascent.net/play

## How to find more (do this after the inbound listings exist)

1. Search YouTube for: `browser MMO`, `text RPG`, `Kingdom of Loathing`, `Fallen London`, `old school MMO`, `play in browser RPG`.
2. Open channels that actually play those games (not recap-shorts farms).
3. Prefer 5k–200k subs with comments from people who play, not 2M “top 10 games” channels.
4. Use the public about-email. One personal sentence (“you played X, this is the shared-tower version of that loop”).
5. Do not mail-merge 200 channels. Ten good emails beat a blast.

## What not to do

- Do not buy “submit to 500 YouTubers” services.
- Do not send Steam-key language. There are no keys.
- Do not mention Luna, AI, or in-game jargon.
- Do not ask for a review. Ask them to play.
