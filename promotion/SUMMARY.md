## The pitch (canonical)

Rules: a stranger should get it in one read. Do not use the game's own words. Do not mention the chat product.

**Tagline**

Looks simple. Takes thousands.

**One line**

A retro text D&D game that looks simple on the screen — and takes thousands of players, together, to take one tower.

**Short**

Linear Ascent looks like the good old times: low resolution, simple text-based D&D. The world underneath is deep. It takes the massive effort of thousands of players, together, to take one tower.

**Medium**

This retro game looks simple — low resolution, text on the screen, like the D&D games from when computers could barely draw a picture. The content is not simple. A whole world lives in those few lines. And nobody takes the tower alone. It takes thousands of players, putting in the work together, to climb one massive tower and bring it down. Free. Nothing to install. https://linearascent.net/play

**Long**

Linear Ascent looks like something from the good old times. Low resolution. Text. A few choices. Old-school D&D on a machine that could barely draw a picture.

That simplicity is only the surface. Underneath, the game is deep — a living world, a long climb, more going on than the screen lets on.

And it takes a crowd. This is a massive online multiplayer. Thousands of players put their effort into the same tower. One climb. One monster. The work of everyone, together, to bring it down.

Free. Nothing to install. Play at https://linearascent.net/play
